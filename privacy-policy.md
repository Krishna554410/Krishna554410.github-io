# Privacy Policy for PDF Editor Pro

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**App Name:** PDF Editor Pro  
**Package Name:** com.mahadev.pdf_editor_pro  

Welcome to **PDF Editor Pro**. Your privacy and document security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

---

## 1. 100% On-Device Document & PDF Processing
* **Complete Document Privacy:** All PDF files, images, scanned documents, passwords, and digital signatures that you view, create, merge, split, compress, lock, unlock, or edit inside **PDF Editor Pro** are processed **100% locally on your device**.
* **No File Uploads:** We **never** upload, store, or transmit your personal PDF documents or files to any external server or cloud storage. Your files remain strictly on your phone.

---

## 2. Information We Collect (Account & Cloud Sync)
To provide reward coins, wallet backup, and VIP membership synchronization across devices, we collect minimal account information when you sign in:
* **Google Sign-In Data:** Your verified Gmail address and display name.
* **User Profile Details:** Your name and age (entered during registration).
* **Wallet & Membership Data:** Your coin balance, transaction history (Hisab-Kitab ledger), VIP plan status, expiry date, and 12-digit UPI Reference/UTR numbers for payment verification. All this data is securely stored and synced using **Google Firebase Realtime Database**.

---

## 3. Payments & VIP Subscriptions (UPI)
* **Direct UPI Transactions:** When you purchase a VIP Pass (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime) or Coin Top-Up Pack, payments are processed through your preferred UPI application (such as PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or BHIM) or merchant QR/UPI ID.
* **Zero Banking Credentials Collected:** We **never** collect or store your bank account numbers, debit/credit card numbers, or UPI PINs. We only record the 12-digit UPI Reference / UTR Number and Order ID on Firebase Cloud to verify your transaction and activate your VIP plan.

---

## 4. Third-Party Advertising Partners (AdMob & Unity Ads)
Our app offers free PDF tools supported by rewarded video advertisements. When you choose to watch a video ad to earn free coins, our third-party advertising partners may collect and use your device's **Advertising ID (GAID)**, IP address, and basic device diagnostics to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud:
* **Google AdMob:** [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Unity Ads:** [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)

VIP Pass members enjoy an ad-free experience in accordance with their active subscription tier.

---

## 5. Device Permissions We Request
* **Storage / Media Access:** Required only so you can select PDF files or images from your phone and save edited PDFs back to your device.
* **Camera Permission:** Required only when you use the built-in **Document Scanner** tool to scan physical pages into PDF.
* **Internet Access:** Required for Google Sign-In, syncing your coin wallet/VIP status with Firebase Cloud, and loading rewarded video ads.

---

## 6. Data Security & Account Deletion Rights
* **Anti-Tamper & Encryption:** Your wallet and account records are protected with cryptographic signatures and transmitted over secure HTTPS connections to Firebase.
* **Data Deletion Request:** You can log out of your account at any time from the app's Settings screen. If you wish to permanently delete your account data and wallet records from our Firebase database, please send an email request to our support address below, and your data will be deleted within 7 business days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our application is intended for users aged 10 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 10.
